💡 About TalentTop

TalentTop is a learning platform built especially for kids who want to take their first steps into the world of programming. Instead of long lessons and theory, kids learn by doing — solving short, fun coding challenges designed for their level.

👶

Who is it for?

Kids who are curious about coding, from complete beginners to those with some experience.

🧩

How it works

Pick a challenge, write your solution, and submit it for review — simple as that.

🎓

Why challenges?

Learning by solving real problems builds confidence and sticks better than just reading theory.
